Research

Peer-reviewed article, Array (Elsevier), 2026

A novel nature inspired feature optimization algorithm to detect breast cancer using machine learning

Rahman, M. A. and Mondal, M. R. H. (2026). Array, 29, 100690. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.array.2026.100690

98.24% accuracy on WBCD and 95.96% on METABRIC, each with 16 selected features.

The paper introduces Fire-Gray Optimization (FGO), which combines the Firefly algorithm and the Gray Wolf Optimizer in sequence to balance exploration and exploitation in feature selection. On WBCD a voting classifier detects breast cancer. On METABRIC the model predicts 5-year survival status. FGO was evaluated for convergence, robustness, bias, and internal validity.

Ongoing research

FedVProp: silent propagation of adversarial influence in federated intrusion detection

2026 to present. Manuscript in preparation with Prof. Rubaiyat Hossain Mondal.

Investigates whether adversarial influence introduced at one federated intrusion-detection client reaches honest clients through repeated model aggregation. Under increasing and oscillatory perturbations, up to 88.89% of honest clients were affected on TON_IoT and 44.44% on IDS Dataset 2025. Counterfactual analysis shows that removing compromised updates significantly improves federated learning outcomes, and the vulnerability profile changes from one dataset to another.

  • Federated learning
  • Intrusion detection
  • Adversarial robustness

Vulnerability propagation in federated intrusion detection on CIC-IDS2018

Exploratory study, unpublished

Examines adversarial noise perturbation across federated clients under FedAvg aggregation. The write-up reports its experimental confounds openly.

XAI-GuardNet: explainable federated learning framework on TON_IoT

Research code (Colab notebook), unpublished

Combines network traffic, Windows and Linux logs, and IoT telemetry. Features are selected in three stages (mutual information, XGBoost, SHAP). Training uses FedAvg with a homogeneous TabTransformer, adversarial defense (FGSM and PGD), differential privacy through DP-SGD, and threat hunting mapped to MITRE ATT&CK.

  • Explainability
  • Differential privacy
  • Multi-modal data
