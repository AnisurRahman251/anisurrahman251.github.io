01
MSc Thesis · Graduate Research · 2022–Present

A Nature-Inspired Feature Optimization Algorithm for Breast Cancer Detection

Designed and validated a hybrid Fruit-fly Group Optimization (FGO) feature-selection algorithm to improve breast cancer classification accuracy while reducing computational overhead. Applied across METABRIC and Wisconsin Breast Cancer Dataset (WBCD), demonstrating significant gains over conventional methods. Evaluated for convergence speed, robustness, algorithmic bias, and internal validity.

≥98% Classification Accuracy 40% Fewer Computational Operations Elsevier · Array · 2026
